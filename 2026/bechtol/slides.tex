  \begin{frame}{Summary}
    \begin{enumerate}
      \item Teams use analytics to win more games through \alert{\bf player evaluation}, \alert{\bf player development}, and \alert{\bf in-game strategy}
      \item Improved strategy is \alert{\bf sometimes less} aesthetically pleasing, \alert{\bf sometimes more} aesthetically pleasing
      \item Some sports (e.g., baseball, volleyball) are more amenable to analysis than others (e.g., soccer)
    \end{enumerate}
  \end{frame}
